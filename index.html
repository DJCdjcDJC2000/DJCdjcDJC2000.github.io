Research

I work at the intersection of numerical analysis, optimization, stochastic dynamical systems, and high-quality computational implementation. I care about methods that are not only mathematically meaningful, but also reproducible and inspectable.

Complementarity systems Dynamic and stochastic linear complementarity problems, variational inequalities, and nonsmooth systems.
Spectral methods Chebyshev and ultraspherical discretizations for structured time-domain computation.

Fast solvers for time-varying dynamic stochastic linear complementarity problems

Current doctoral research · Applied mathematics and scientific computing

Active

Developing a solver framework that combines Fischer-Burmeister smoothing, QMC approximation, ultraspherical spectral discretization, and waveform iteration for DSLCPs. The current emphasis is on clear error structure, fair numerical comparison, and reproducible implementation.

DSLCP Fischer-Burmeister smoothing QMC Ultraspherical spectral methods MATLAB / Python

Sparse spectral methods for constrained optimal control problems

Master's thesis and manuscript work · Harbin Institute of Technology

Manuscript

Proposed a modal Chebyshev spectral discretization strategy for constrained optimal control problems, converting dynamic constraints into sparse structured coefficient equations and reducing the burden of dense node-based pseudospectral formulations.

Optimal control Chebyshev polynomials Sparse discretization Nonlinear programming
