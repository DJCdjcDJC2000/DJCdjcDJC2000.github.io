Numerical methods for nonsmooth stochastic systems

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Applied Mathematics at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

My current research develops fast numerical methods for differential stochastic linear complementarity problems. The work combines smoothing methods, sample average approximation, ultraspherical spectral discretization, and block iterative solvers, with an emphasis on structured error analysis and accuracy-cost evaluation.

DSLCPs Differential stochastic linear complementarity problems.
Smoothing Fischer-Burmeister smoothing and sample approximation.
Spectral Ultraspherical methods for time-domain computation.
Solvers Block iterative methods and reproducible experiments.

Research

Research interests

I am interested in numerical methods whose mathematical structure and computational behavior can be analyzed together.

Complementarity systems

Differential and stochastic linear complementarity problems, nonsmooth dynamical systems, and variational structures.

Approximation

Smoothing methods, sample average approximation, and finite-sample stochastic feedback terms.

Spectral methods

Chebyshev and ultraspherical spectral discretization for structured time-domain computation.

Scientific computing

Block iterative solvers, accuracy-cost evaluation, and reproducible numerical protocols.
